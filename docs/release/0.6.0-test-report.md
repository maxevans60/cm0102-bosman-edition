# CM 01/02: Bosman Edition 0.6.0 test report

0.6.0 adds every season from 2001/02 to 2025/26 to the game. Each season has its own database, leagues, cups and rules, and in a long game the rules change each July. This report says how the release was tested, what the tests found and fixed, which safety nets stay in the game, and the known limits.

[PENDING] marks a result of the final test runs on the release build. Those runs are not finished yet.

## 1. How 0.6.0 was tested

### Season jump runs

The game played a long career from each of the 25 start seasons, 2001/02 to 2025/26, with every nation selected and changing rules on. Five more starts ran with changing rules off (2001, 2006, 2011, 2016 and 2021), and one 2014 start added the Italian Serie C2. The quick season jump plays the matches without the match engine.

- Each run goes on to 1 September after one World Cup and one European Championship with their qualifiers. Together the runs cover 88 seasons.
- Near each 1 July the game saves, and a new copy of the program loads that save and plays on.
- A run fails on any error box.
- A run also fails when a league or a cup stops moving on to its next season. This check came in after a test run passed while the Italian and Danish leagues had stopped with no error box.

### Full play runs

Five careers ran with the match engine on, all nations and changing rules on, with the manager on holiday. Each one saves and loads each July and goes on to September after a World Cup and a European Championship.

### Same game checks

The base game is 2001/02 with the 3.9.68 data and changing rules off. In the base game the new season code must change nothing. At every merge four sets of test games ran with the season code on and off, and the saves were compared byte for byte. With changing rules on, a game may differ only in the parts that the rules are meant to change.

### Platforms

- Mac: the smoke test in classic and widescreen, and a screen sweep of 2001 to the end of 2004/05.
- Windows: the smoke test, a season change with a save and a load, and a run to the end of 2004/05, all under wine.
- Linux: the AppImage build and its file list, the smoke test, and a season change with a save and a load.
- The release dry run builds and checks all three downloads.

### Other checks

- Saves above 2 GB: a 3.3 GB save loads, and a save that would pass 4 GB is refused while the earlier save stays as it was.
- Memory: a 100-season Select All game fits in the game's memory.
- Each fix had its own retest before it was merged.

## 3. Faults found and fixed

The test runs found these faults. Each one has a fix at its cause and a retest.

Error boxes:

- National squads short of players after a load, and under-21 squads that took players from the senior squad of the same international window.
- A ban held in the wrong nation after Australia moved to Asia, and a ban held for a player on loan between an Irish and a Northern Irish club in the All-Ireland cup.
- Draws of World Cup and European Championship qualifying with a missing team, a host in qualifying, or a play-off tie built too late.
- A Europa League club with only 10 players in the knockout rounds, because it kept its August squad.
- A Club World Championship whose knockout never ended after a load.
- A player on loan who lost his contract with his parent club when his loan club took him off its staff.
- The Finalissima, the 2019 Confederations Cup with changing rules off, the Portuguese Cup of a 2021/22 start, an EFL Trophy news item, and a national squad that named a player twice.
- An FA Cup tie with a garbled club in a 2025/26 start.
- A club that rotated its best players out of a European match when too few could play, and a reserve side with no goalkeeper after its club loaned its keepers out.
- Saves above 2 GB that would not load, and a Select All game that ran out of memory in its ninth season. Both are faults of the original game too.

Leagues and cups that stopped:

- Serie A and Serie B after 2015/16.
- The Danish leagues, which waited a year when a match moved past their start day.
- The Greek Super League 2 South with changing rules off.
- The Leinster and Munster Senior Cups and the Polish Cup, which kept clubs from the last edition.
- The four-club Supercopa de España when one club held two places.
- The Russian Cup after a final played after 1 June.
- The Coppa Italia Serie C when its group size changed.
- Japan's Emperor's Cup, when a tie made after its date fell after the next round's match day.

Competitions and dates:

- The Segunda División play-off was often skipped for lack of time, and a level final went to replays.
- UEFA ties moved because players were away on the original's 2001 international dates, so the next draws came late. Friendlies, qualifiers and UEFA matches now follow the real calendars from the second season of a game.
- The knockout rounds of the UEFA cups used one set of dates in every season.
- The Champions Path and the League Path mixed in Champions League qualifying.
- League One and League Two held fewer clubs than in real life in some seasons, so the English cups were one club short.
- 55 rows of the club tables pointed at the wrong club, for example in Turkey and France.

Data and downloads:

- Summer 2004 and Summer 2006 had people who appeared twice. The release has the corrected files.
- The release tool did not read the log of the current Windows installer version, and the Linux build ran out of memory in the release container.

## 4. Safety nets

Where an error box came from a fault, the fix removes the cause. In some places a guard stays beside the fix as a safety net. A net acts only where the fault would come back, and every net writes a line to the game's log when it acts. In the test runs a net that acts counts as a failure, and its cause is then fixed.

These nets stay, each beside a fix of its cause:

- National squads: a squad still short of players at match time is filled from the nation's players.
- Domestic cups: an empty place in a cup is filled from the clubs of that nation. Cups that never play keep their fill.
- Club cups of the confederations: a place with no club is filled when a database holds too few clubs of a continent.
- World Cup and European Championship: a finals list that is short after qualifying is filled, and a team that is in already stays out.
- The match engine: a player with no valid place on the pitch is placed as the original's own code does.
- Bans: a held ban of the wrong ruling body gets a new entry.
- Leagues: a promotion and relegation count that does not fit the division's size is logged and dropped.
- UEFA qualifying: a round drawn after its first-leg date moves that leg a few days on.
- Loans: a loan that ends with no parent contract is freed.
- Screens: a screen area that asks for more than 30 rows becomes a scrolling list. Long Competitions menus, such as Brazil's, have a sub menu More so they stay below that limit.
- Language files: the box for a language file that does not fit the database is skipped.

## 5. Known limits

Play:

- The bench and the substitutes stay at 9 or fewer, because the match engine holds 9.
- The first season of a game keeps the original's international and friendly calendar. The real FIFA days apply from the second season. UEFA qualifying takes its real days in the first season too, except in a 2001/02 start.
- In the base game the MLS play-offs keep the original's run-in shootout, which can still reach an error box in a very long run-in. Season games use extra time and penalties.
- The Turkish Category A plays 20 clubs where the real league had 18, because the Turkish Cup takes its entrants from fixed places.
- The Danish First Division plays 16 clubs in the seasons where the database holds 16, not the real 12, because the Danish Cup needs its full count of entrants.
- Small nations have fewer than 22 eligible under-21 players, so the game adds its own virtual players at match time.
- Bonaire, French Guiana, Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint-Martin and Sint Maarten stay out of the CONCACAF Nations League, because the databases give them no players.
- The 2030 World Cup places use each confederation's 2026 total, with its hosts counted in it, until a database holds the real allocation.
- The Euro 2028 host places follow the announced rule. The ranking of the hosts by group place and the play-off entrants by Nations League ranking are our reading of it.
- A draw can still put a club against the winner of a tie that holds a club of its own nation, as in the original game.
- The National League Cup from 2024/25 is not played, because its real entrants include under-21 sides that the game does not have.
- The competitions that 0.6.0 adds show their names in English in every language.

Tests:

- Windows ran only under wine on a Mac, not on real Windows. The Windows run used England only.
- Linux had no season jump run to a World Cup and a European Championship.
- No test ran on a Steam Deck.
- A language change during a career through the Options screen was not tested.
- Saves above 2 GB were tested on the Mac only.
- A save grows by about 30 MB a season. It reaches about 3 GB at 100 seasons and the 4 GB limit at about 130.
