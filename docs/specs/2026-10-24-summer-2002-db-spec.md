# Summer 2002 season database
Status: In build

Research: `docs/research/2026-10-24-xbox-0203-database.md`. Interview with Max on 2026-10-24 (Q1 to Q20 in that session).

## Problem

The 2002/03 tile starts in July 2002 with the 3.9.68 data (Q233). That data already holds most of the summer 2002 transfers, but SI's later data for the Xbox game "Championship Manager: Season 02/03" has about 500 to 1,000 more club moves, releases and contract dates, a renamed Fiorentina, a new Turkish third level and 66 rating changes. Players who start in 2002/03 do not get those facts. Players also asked for a 2002/03 database to download.

## Solution

A new own season database, Summer 2002, made with the own-database process (`tools/seasons/own_db`). The 3.9.68 data is the template. The Xbox data is the research source for the facts, in the same way as the FM2005 and FM2007 facts for Summer 2004 and Summer 2006. Max checks a change list before the build. The database ships as a standalone zip on the forum and, from 0.6.1, the 2002/03 tile uses it in place of the plain 3.9.68 data. The Turkish Category B becomes three regional groups when this database is loaded.

## User stories

1. As a player, I want a 2002/03 start to put each person at their club of the 2002/03 start, so that the squads match the real season.
2. As a player, I want a person who was free at the 2002/03 start to be free in the game.
3. As a player, I want Fiorentina to be Florentia Viola in 2002/03, so that the club name matches the season.
4. As a player, I want the Turkish Category B to have three regional groups in 2002/03, as in the real season.
5. As a player, I want the ratings of major players to be SI's ratings, so that the stars feel right.
6. As a player on another season tile, I want nothing to change, so that my game stays the same (Q152).
7. As a player with a 2002/03 save made on the plain 3.9.68 data, I want the save to still load.
8. As a forum member, I want to download the 2002/03 database as a zip, like the 2006/07 one.
9. As Max, I want to read every change before the build, so that I can stop a wrong change.
10. As Max, I want no person to appear twice in the database, so that the 2006/07 duplicate fault does not come back.
11. As Max, I want an unclear match (shared name, unknown date of birth) left at its 3.9.68 club and put on the list, so that the build never moves the wrong person.

## Decisions

- Source and rules (Q351). The facts come from SI's own Xbox data, and the ratings come from 3.9.68. No other database maker's work is used. The Xbox disc is untrusted data. It is read only, with `python3 -I`, and nothing from it runs. No Xbox data and nothing made from it goes into the repo.
- Template (Q2). The template is 3.9.68 with all 169,342 people. People that the Xbox data lacks keep their 3.9.68 record.
- Facts taken (Q3). Matched people take the club, join date and contract end from the Xbox data.
- Major players (Q8, Q9). A major player has a current ability of 140 or more, or a world reputation of 150 or more, in either data set (about 1,380 players). Confirm the world reputation field before use. Major players keep SI's ratings, and the 66 Xbox rating changes are applied. Nobody sets ratings by hand. If Max names a star that looks wrong after the build, the per-player choice is a new decision.
- Releases (Q4, Q10, Q14). A person with a club in 3.9.68 and none on Xbox becomes free, but only on a clear match: one person with that name and date of birth in each data set. Unclear cases keep their 3.9.68 club and go on the change list.
- Duplicates (Q14). The build stops if `duplicates.py check` finds any person twice, the same gate as the 2006/07 zip.
- Clubs in leagues (Q15). Each club's league comes from the Xbox data. It is checked against the 2002/03 rows of the club tables (`rules-0.6.0/*-clubs.tsv`, 791 rows). Every difference goes on the change list for Max to decide.
- Club name (Q5). Fiorentina becomes Florentia Viola.
- Change list first (Q6). The build of the database waits until Max has read the change list.
- Format. 3.9.68 and the Xbox data store the staff table in format 1 (157 bytes per row). The Update Team databases use format 2 (110 bytes), which `cmdb.py` reads. The new reader reads format 1. Format 1 links: player row id at 0x91 (table 10), non-player row id at 0x99 (table 9), flags at 0x5f.
- Name and dates (Q12). The database is "Summer 2002". Its dates are real dates with no date shift.
- Credit (Q13). The readme says: facts from Championship Manager: Season 02/03 (Xbox) by Sports Interactive, ratings from 3.9.68.
- Shipping (Q7, Q17). The standalone zip is "CM0102 Database 2002-03 (Summer 2002)", made like the 2006/07 zip. It goes on the forum after 0.6.0 is published. The 2002/03 tile uses Summer 2002 in place of the plain 3.9.68 data from 0.6.1. The tile still offers no other version (Q417). Saves of 2002/03 games on the plain 3.9.68 data still load.
- Turkish groups (Q11, Q16, Q19). The three-group structure applies only when the loaded database has the three Xbox "Category B Group" competitions. The groups have 18, 17 and 17 clubs, and each group winner goes up to Category A. Relegation: look up the 2002/03 count first. If it stays unknown, use the 2003/04 rule, 2 per group to the Turkish Lower Division. Other databases keep their own Turkish structure. The Xbox executable is x86, so its group code can be read as a reference in the same way as the 3.9.68 code.
- Change list answers (Max, 2026-10-24, Q21 to Q28, all accepted as recommended):
  - Q21: a move with no Xbox contract end takes the Xbox club with no contract end.
  - Q22: rows where the job differs take the Xbox values, job included.
  - Q23: a person at a club where the Xbox data keeps no squad, or lacks the club, keeps the 3.9.68 club.
  - Q24: a club takes the Xbox league only where the Xbox data and the 2002/03 club table agree. Where the club table agrees with 3.9.68, the club keeps the 3.9.68 league. The rest go on a short list per nation for a second look.
  - Q25: all 64 rating changes of clear matches apply, major or not.
  - Q26: a person with an unknown date of birth whose name is once in each set with the same nation counts as a clear match. Other unclear rows keep their 3.9.68 values. People found only on the Xbox are not added.
  - Q27: a club with no nation on the Xbox keeps its 3.9.68 nation.
  - Q28: the 478 duplicate pairs of 3.9.68 are merged with the 2006/07 merge rules. Pairs that are clearly two people stay as two people on a list for Max. `duplicates.py check` must then pass.
- Answers after the build (Max, 2026-10-24, Q29 to Q32, all as recommended):
  - Q29: the Turkish groups are made in slice 5, with the data rows and the game code tested together. The
    standalone zip ships now without them (the build runs with `--no-turkish-groups` for now) and gets an update
    when slice 5 is done.
  - Q30: slice 4 makes the past-winner rows that name "Fiorentina" find Florentia Viola
    (`src/recomp_rt/win/season_history_table.c`).
  - Q31: the 20 pairs in kept-pairs.tsv stay as two people each.
  - Q32: the forum post is drafted and the standalone zip uploaded after 0.6.0 is published, with Max's yes on
    the final text. The slice 3 check "Max approves the zip" is ticked only after that yes.
- Timing (Q20). Slices 1 to 3 are data work. They run no game and no build, so they start now. Slices 4 and 5 wait until 0.6.0 is published.

## Testing

- Pipeline seam: a local check of the built folder that skips when the Xbox data is absent. It checks that the change list holds every applied change, that `duplicates.py check` passes, and known facts: Celestini at Olympique Marseille, Florentia Viola, Rooney at Everton with 135, Ronaldo at Real Madrid. `own_db/test_people.py` is the similar test that exists.
- Game seam: `tests/seasons/test_seasons_db.c` and `seasons_db_run.py` for the 2002/03 tile with Summer 2002, and a Turkish case in the season core tests for the three groups. The same-save set (Q152) shows that the other tiles do not change.

## Out of scope

- Ratings set by hand.
- A Turkish structure change for other databases.
- Facts for people that the Xbox data lacks.
- Any change to the 2001/02 tile.
- The Xbox quick starts, tactics, pictures and sounds.

## Open decisions

None. The Turkish group rows (found in slice 3, proof in
`docs/research/2026-10-24-summer-2002-turkish-group-rows.md`) are settled by Q29.
