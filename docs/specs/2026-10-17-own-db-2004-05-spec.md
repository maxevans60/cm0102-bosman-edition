# Own season database 2004/05
Status: Database approved by Max (2026-10-17); registration on this branch, not merged

Parent spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-16-seasons-0.6.0-spec.md` (Q351). Sister work: the 2006/07 database from FM2007 facts (its own session and branch).

## Problem
No Update Team database exists for 2004/05, so the 2004/05 tile of the Choose Season screen shows "No database". Q351 says that we build our own: the facts from Football Manager 2005, whose data is the 2004/05 season, the ratings and positions from the nearest Update Team database, and public sources for the rest.

## Solution
A converter takes the September 2005 Update Team database as the template and writes the FM2005 facts into it: who plays and works at which club, contracts, joined dates and dates of birth. The 2004/05 divisions come from FM2005's 2003/04 final tables plus the real promotions, relegations and play-off winners. The output is a normal 3.9.68 data folder that stores its dates the Update Team way (Q381). The 2006/07 database uses the same converter core with its own season settings.

## User stories
1. As a player who picks 2004/05, I want a database, so that I can start a game in that season.
2. As a player, I want the squads of summer 2004 (Drogba at Chelsea, Rooney at Manchester United), so that the season feels right.
3. As a player, I want every age and year of birth to be right on the first day.
4. As a player, I want the 2004/05 divisions as they were, with the 2003/04 champions in the history.
5. As Max, I want no rating from FM2005 in the database, so that the ratings stay the Update Team's.
6. As a maintainer, I want one converter for 2004/05 and 2006/07, so that a fix helps both seasons.

## Decisions (Max, 2026-10-17, Q380 to Q386; numbered from Q380 because the 2006/07 session may use Q352 on)
- Q380: the template is the September 2005 Update Team database. Ratings, positions, the 1,235 key names and all clubs come from it. FM2005 sets clubs, jobs, contracts, joined dates and dates of birth.
- Q381 (Max, given in the maintainer: "Update team"): the database stores its dates the Update Team way, as for a 2001 start: dates of birth, joined dates, contract ends, national job dates and careers are 3 years early; competition history and officials keep their real years. It then works as a known season database, as a Custom database and in the original game. Its signature row, added after Max approves the database, carries these shifts (birth, joined, contract and careers 3; the rest 0).
- Q382: a FM2005 player who is not in September 2005 takes his ratings and position from October 2003.
- Q383: the 2006/07 rules apply (ratings rule changed by Max on 2026-10-17: see Rebuild). Ratings come from the Update Team databases (they consented). A player that no Update Team database has is left out, and so is a player with no date of birth, except key players of the CM 01/02 playable nations: for those the position comes from public sources and the ratings are our own, cross-checked against FM2005's so that they are not far off. FM2005's ratings never go into the output.
- Q384: the September 2005 ratings stay as they are, one season later.
- Q385: the 2004/05 divisions come from FM2005's 2003/04 final tables and the real promotions, relegations and play-off winners, each with a URL. Division sizes are the template's.
- Q386: the 2006/07 converter becomes the shared core with the season as a parameter. This branch adds the FM2005 reader and the 2004/05 settings. Tools only, no data.
- Q371 (Max, 2026-10-18): the key-player list keeps the English, Scottish and Dutch second tiers (the division names that end in "First Division").
- Q372 (Max, 2026-10-18): Wexford Youths stays a filler club until slice 15b.

## Rules
- FM2005 is untrusted data. Nothing from the disc runs. It is read as data with `python3 -I`, in `/Users/max/Projects/cm0102/fm2005-work/`.
- No database, FM data or anything made from it goes into the repo. No work of another database maker is used, and no other modder is named.
- Every format fact comes from the 3.9.68 code, the repo research or the Update Team data.

## Results (2026-10-17)
Build: `read_fm2005.py`, then `match.py`, `leagues.py` and `convert.py` of `tools/seasons/own_db/` with season 2004 (work folder `fm2005-work/build.sh`).
- FM2005: 256,675 people, 181,593 with a club, 38,817 rows of final tables (1889 to 2004), 16,422 rows of competition winners.
- Matching: 128,783 FM people to the template. Many template people have no date of birth (53,295 rows with year 0 or 1900), so people also match by name within the same club. 218 FM players come from October 2003 (Q382).
- Divisions: England from the 2003/04 tables and the four play-off winners, exact. 32 other divisions from their real 2004/05 lists. Italy and France have 20 clubs but the game has 18 places, so the two lowest promoted clubs stay down. Kerkyra (Greece) is not in the template.
- Game: `update_team_sweep.py` passes with `RECOMP_START_SEASON=2004`, as a Custom database (main build; the game measures +3 for 114,469 of 114,869 people) and as a known database with a local signature row with the shifts of Q381 (worktree build, not committed). The first news is on 10.7.2004 with the 2004/05 fixture list. Arsenal's squad is that of summer 2004. Henry is 26 (born 17.8.77) and Fàbregas 17 (born 4.5.87).

## Rebuild (2026-10-17 night, branch own-db-rebuild)
Both seasons were rebuilt in one change, with new signature rows and the same date shifts.
- Squad lists: 275 template lists start with -1 and hold ids further on; convert.py kept them, so about 2,000
  entries named players of other clubs (and clubs whose template list is empty got no list for their players) (pick_team 0x6dbc50 stops at match_man..cpp 1225 when one player comes
  twice through a B team, 0x540a50, check 0x6dd3a4). They are rebuilt now, and convert.py stops if one is left.
- Last division and place: FM's final tables also hold the rows of reserve sides, whose team ids belong to the
  parent club. A reserve row replaced the club's own row, so ten 2004/05 Bundesliga clubs had "A Lower Division"
  as last division, and the German league cup (top six of last season's First Division, collect_entrants 0x5dc700)
  stopped at ger_lge_cup.cpp 285 with Germany selected. leagues.py now takes only first-team rows (the first team id
  of each club record), and convert.py checks the last places 1 to 6 of the German First Division. The other league
  cups take clubs by division or reputation.
- Reserve sides: FM keeps a club's reserve side as its second team id, and its players have the senior club as
  contract club. Reserve records of the template (for example "FC Bayern München Amateure", "Real Madrid C.F. B")
  were empty, and the game pairs them with the senior club (club_get_linked_b_team 0x540a50); with Germany selected
  a game stopped at match_man..cpp 1225 in July. FM's second-team players now go to the reserve record
  (seasons.reserve_teams), and a reserve record that would keep fewer than 16 players keeps its template players.
  German reserve players with no record now count at their reserve side, so they leave the key-player list
  (2006/07: 36 key players, before 51).
- Playable squads: a side needs 11 players (CM3_MATCH_ENGINE::init_match 0x6b7f20 stops when the side set-up
  0x6db500 returns fewer, cmp al, 0xb at 0x6b886b and 0x6b89be). A played division is one whose clubs all have 11
  players in the template. A club of a played division, or a reserve record (also "Jong ..."), that would keep
  fewer than 16 players keeps its unmatched template players, then gets back its template players with no club,
  then takes players with no club of its nation. convert.py stops if a club of a played division names fewer
  than 11 players. A club that a division swap moved out now has no division (C.P. Granada 74 in 2006/07 kept the
  template's Second Division place before).
- Lead decisions for Max to confirm (2026-10-18): the 15 German reserve players count at their reserve clubs and
  leave the key-player list; the reserve fill and the squad check apply to every nation.
- Loans (2004/05): FM2005's STARTING_LOAN table (after COMP_HISTORY in server_db.dat, 27-byte rows). 2,953
  loaned players start at their loan club (Riquelme at Villarreal, Saviola at Monaco).
- Careers: FM's pl_hist files give the rows from 2002/03 to the season before the start for every matched person
  when FM gives a row with a known club for that season; the template's rows of that season go, other seasons keep
  the template's rows.
- Key players (Q383; Max 2026-10-17: FM's rating of each player is the reference, no wage-peer check):
  `keyplayers.py`. 23 players in 2004/05, 36 in 2006/07. A record within one season keeps its Update Team
  ratings. Otherwise the current and potential ability are FM's, mapped by the median Update Team value of the
  matched players with the same FM value, and with slope 1 beyond the values that 100 matched players have (so that the sparse top is not pulled down). The link from an FM person to his ratings row: the u32 after the five s16 values of the ACTUAL_PLAYER
  block (FM2005, from the FM2005 Mac data editor's loaders), the u32 at ACTUAL_PLAYER +0xb0 (FM2007, fm_i386
  0x8067ba). The list rule stays (top-division clubs by name, FM wage 1,000 or more). A rule on FM's current
  ability (at least the club's 18th best) gave about 3,000 players a season, most at clubs with thin squads outside
  the playable nations.
- Clubs: Kerkyra (2004/05) is the template's "Kerkira". In 2006/07 Gençlerbirliği OFTAŞ, Sibir Novosibirsk and
  Spartak-MZhK Ryazan are template records under older names (the same record ids carry the real names in later
  Update Team databases). C.F. Ciudad de Murcia gets its nation and stadium back. Only Wexford Youths (founded
  2007) has no record: a filler takes its slot.

## Final rebuild (2026-10-19)
- The Summer 2004 stop at match_man..cpp 1225 with Germany selected: the team pick 0x6dbc50 takes its candidates
  from the squad list (side +0x27, which init_match sets to club +0xd7 at 0x6b8850), and in every caught case
  Eintracht Frankfurt's list named Andreas Menger twice. He is a player-coach (job 15). The templates put a
  player-manager (12), player-assistant (13) and player-coach (15) in the staff slot of the job and in the squad
  list, as database_add_staff_to_club 0x53bd10 does (jump table 0x53c14c). convert.py put them in the squad list
  only, so the load did not link Menger to the club, and CONTRACT_MANAGER::toggle_squad_placement 0x4cbd20 added him
  to the list a second time (call at 0x4cc091) when it moved him between the club and its B side. It also put
  managers, assistants, coaches and scouts with player data (jobs 5, 6, 8, 9) in the squad list, where the templates
  have none.
- convert.py now builds the lists by job (`JOB_LISTS`, `in_squad`), and `check_squad_lists` stops when a person is
  not in the slot of his job or the squad list names someone whose job takes no squad slot.
- Small decision (recommended answer taken): a player-manager or player-assistant whose club already has FM's
  manager or assistant leaves the club, as a second manager did before (2004/05: 30 more people leave a list,
  2006/07: 31).
- Both seasons were rebuilt (two seeds, identical files), with new signature rows. The date shifts stay as decided;
  only the measured counts changed. The history fill and the club rows are unchanged. The 2006/07 data changed, so
  the 2006/07 forum package was rebuilt and must be posted again.
