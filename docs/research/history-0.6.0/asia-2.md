# History research, part asia-2

Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand. 106 rows in `asia-2.tsv`: 94 `yes`, 5 `not held`, 7 `unknown`. Sources read on 2026-10-17. The `ended` column is `unknown` when no source gives the date of the last match.

## Label conventions, with the 3.9.68 rows that prove them

### Season ends in the label year

- Hong Kong First Division: 2002 Sun Hei, Happy Valley = 2001/02. 2001 Happy Valley, Instant-Dict = 2000/01 (RSSSF list of champions).
- Hong Kong FA Cup: 2002 South China, Sun Hei = 2001/02. 2001 Instant-Dict, South China = 2000/01.
- Hong Kong League Cup: 2002 South China, Rangers = 2001/02. 2001 Happy Valley, Sun Hei = 2000/01.
- Hong Kong Junior Challenge Shield: 2002 Mutual = 2001/02. 2001 Fukien = 2000/01.
- Hong Kong Second Division: 2002 Fukien, Tung Po = 2001/02. 2000 Tung Po, HKFC = 1999/2000.
- Hong Kong Third Division A and B: 2002 Kwok Keung = 2001/02 (zh Wikipedia lists Kwok Keung as 2001/02 third division champion). 2001 Div. B PE Teachers = 2000/01 (zh list).
- Indian National Football League: 1997 JCT = 1996/97, 2001 East Bengal = 2000/01, 2002 Mohun Bagan = 2001/02.

### Season starts in the label year

- Hong Kong Senior Challenge Shield: 1996 South China, Instant-Dict = 1996/97. 1997 Happy Valley, Sing Tao = 1997/98. 1999 South China, Happy Valley = 1999/2000. 2000 Orient and Yee Hope Union, Instant-Dict = 2000/01. 2001 South China, Sun Hei = 2001/02. The finals were played in the spring of the second year. So label 2002 is 2002/03, and label 2005 is 2005/06, which ended in March 2006.
- Thai Premier League: 1996 Bangkok Bank, Stock Exchange = 1996/97. 2001 BEC Tero-Sasana, Osothsapa = 2001/02. Label 2002 is 2002/03, 2003 is 2003/04 (started 27 December 2003), 2004 is 2004/05 (started 28 November 2004). No season started in 2005, because the 2006 season was a calendar-year season. Label 2005 is `not held`.

### Calendar year

- Malaysia (all five): 2001 Malaysia Cup Terengganu beat Perak, Charity Shield 2001 Terengganu, Perak, FA Cup 2001 Selangor, Sarawak, Liga Perdana 1 2001 Pulau Pinang.
- Singapore (S.League, Singapore Cup, Prime League): 2001 S.League Geylang, SAF. 2001 Cup Home United, Geylang.
- Indonesia: 1995 to 2000 hold the split seasons by end year (1995 Persib = 1994/95, 2000 PSM = 1999/2000). 2001 Persija = the 2001 season. From 2001 the league is a calendar season.
- Indian Federation Cup and Super Cup: each edition is named by a year. Federation Cup 2001 Mohun Bagan, Dempo and Super Cup 1999 Salgaocar match the 3.9.68 rows.
- Indian regional leagues: Calcutta 2001 Mohun Bagan, Goa 2001 Churchill and Salgaocar (RSSSF 2001 final table), Delhi 1995 to 2000, Bangalore 1995 to 2001, Guwahati 1998 to 2001, Punjab 1998 to 2000 and Kerala 1998 and 1999 all match the calendar-year lists. Kerala 1999 was played in December 1999. Mumbai 2000 = the 2000 Harwood League.

## Points of doubt

- Mumbai and Delhi. Each had a 2001-02 edition followed by a 2002 edition. I put the 2001-02 edition on label 2001 and the 2002 edition on label 2002. This is an inference. The Mumbai 2001-02 Elite Division ended in January 2002. The second source calls the Delhi 2002 edition "2002-03".
- Kerala 2003. The 2003-04 Kerala Football League (State Bank of Travancore beat FC Kochin) has no known date, so I cannot give it label 2003 or 2004. I wrote `unknown`.
- Hong Kong Third Division B. From 2002/03 the Third Division was split into Third "A" (club teams) and Third "District" (district teams). RSSSF calls the District league "Third Division B" in 2002/03. I mapped the data's Third Division B to it. The data's Third Division A maps to Third "A".
- Hong Kong 2001/02 lower divisions. RSSSF has the tables only as of 29 March 2002. The 2002 rows for Third Division A (Kwok Keung first) and B (Ornaments, Hi Lee) rest on those tables and on the zh list. The Third Division A runner-up is unknown.
- Hong Kong Third Division A 2003/04 and 2004/05: no final tables found. The 2004/05 table is incomplete. The zh list gives Derico Friends as the overall third division champion in 2004/05, but that list mixes A and District, so I did not use it.
- Indian Super Cup 2003: Wikipedia names it 2003, RSSSF names it "Super Cup 2003/04". It was played on 19 November 2003.
- Bangalore 2005: Wikipedia says 2005, the second source says 2005/6.
- Singapore President's Cup: the President's Cup is the old name of the Singapore FA Cup. Since 1999 that cup has been for clubs below the S.League. The rows give the FA Cup finals. The 2005 winner is SAFSA, the Armed Forces sports association, not the S.League club Singapore Armed Forces FC.
- Malaysian League Premier One and Two: from 2004 the levels are the Super League and the Liga Premier. Premier One is researched to 2003 only. For Premier Two in 2004 and 2005, the Liga Premier champion and runner-up came from a final between the two group winners.
- Tai Po. The Tai Po district team appears only as "Wofoo Tai Po" in the later databases. I used that name.
- Guwahati 2003. The data spells the club "Assam State Election Board", but the real name is Assam State Electricity Board. I used the data spelling.
