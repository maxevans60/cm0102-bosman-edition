## Points of doubt

1. Indian Kerala Super League. The 2000-01 Kerala Football League (State Bank of Travancore beat Titanium XI) has no known date. It is label 2000 if it was played in 2000, and label 2001 if it was played in 2001. The 2001-02 edition was not held. I wrote `unknown` in all places. If the caller follows the 3.9.68 pattern (1998 = 1998-99, 1999 = 1999-2000 played in December 1999), the 2000-01 edition is label 2000 and label 2001 is `not held`. This is an inference.
2. Indian Mumbai League. The 2001/02 Elite Division (ended 8 January 2002) is on label 2001, as the asia-2 part decided. This is an inference.
3. Norwegian Second Division. The 2. divisjon changed from 8 groups in 2000 to 4 groups in 2001, so groups 5 to 8 are `not held` from label 2001, one year earlier than the nordic-2 part says. Groups 1 to 4 are mapped by group number (inference, as in nordic-2).
4. Portuguese Third Division. In 2000/01 the III Divisão series had champions, but no national final was played (Wikipedia shows "Not Held"). I marked it `not held` and listed the series winners in `notes`. The 3.9.68 data has no 2001 row for it.
5. Irish Leinster Senior Cup. The 2000/01 edition was abandoned (foot-and-mouth disease), so it is `not held`. The 3.9.68 data has an empty row at label 2001. That agrees.
6. Polish Super Cup. RSSSF puts the Super Cup of 15 July 2001 (Wisla Krakow 4-3 Polonia Warszawa) at the end of the 2000/01 page. It is the 2001 match, so it is label 2001.
7. Confederations Cup 2001 had two hosts. I wrote Japan, as the 3.9.68 row of this tournament has it. The nations part wrote the first-named host (South Korea) for the 2002 World Cup. The caller must decide if both rows must use one rule.
8. Ties on points, the higher place by goal difference: Norwegian Second Division Group 1 (Tollnes and FF Lillehammer, 50 pts) and Scottish Third Division (Hamilton Academical and Cowdenbeath, 76 pts).
