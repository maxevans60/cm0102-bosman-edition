# History research: y2001-a (label year 2001)

Part `y2001-a`, slice 11c, extra pass for Q354. Some databases used the label 2001 history row of a competition for their last champions, so the real 2001 row is gone. This part researches label 2001 only, for the 115 competitions in `targets.tsv`.

The rows are in `y2001-a.tsv`:

- 118 rows. The Argentine Premier Division has two rows (Apertura and Clausura). The Inter-Toto Cup has three rows (Winner 1 to 3).
- 101 rows are `yes`, 16 are `not held` and 1 is `unknown` (Indian Haryana State Championship).
- 6 rows have `ended = unknown`.

All pages were read on 2026-10-17 as raw HTML with curl.

## Label conventions

The convention of each competition comes from its research part. Label 2001 is the edition just before the label 2002 edition of that part. The part `.md` files give the proof from the 3.9.68 rows.

| Convention | Label 2001 holds | Competitions |
|---|---|---|
| Season end year | 2000/01 | English, German, Austrian, French, Belgian, Dutch, Czech and Danish leagues and cups, European Champions Cup, UEFA Cup, Asian Club Championship, Asian Cup Winners' Cup |
| Season start year | 2001/02 | Croatian competitions, Greek Cup and Greek National B Division, Australian National Soccer League, Argentine Second Division, English Conference Cup |
| Calendar year | 2001 | Brazilian, Chinese and Finnish competitions, Copa Libertadores, Copa Mercosur, Merconorte Cup, CAF competitions, CIS Champions Cup, Asian Super Cup, Intercontinental Cup |
| Year of the August or July match | the 2001 match | European Super Cup, English Charity Shield, Belgian, Dutch, French and Austrian super cups, German League Cup |
| Two rows per label | Apertura 2001 and Clausura 2002 | Argentine Premier Division |
| Edition year + 1 | the 2000 edition (30 December 2000) | Chinese FA Super Cup (part asia-1) |
| Inference of part asia-2 | the 2001-02 edition | Indian Dehli Senior Division |

## Check against the 3.9.68 rows

The 3.9.68 data holds a label 2001 row for 85 of these competitions. All 85 rows agree with the web result, club for club. There are no other database rows at label 2001 in any `db-rows.tsv` file.

Points about names:

- Belgian Third Division A: the real runner-up of 2000/01 is Zultse VV. That club merged into KSV Zulte Waregem in 2001. I wrote KSV Zulte Waregem, as the 3.9.68 row does.
- Chinese Second Division: Liaoning Youth is in `clubs/none.tsv` (a club with no nation), and is not in `clubs/China.tsv`.

These competitions have no 3.9.68 row at label 2001. The web result is the only source for them:

- Held: African CAF Cup, CIS Champions Cup, European Super Cup, Inter-Toto Cup, Austrian First Division, Brazilian Minas Gerais Lower Division, Brazilian Rio de Janeiro Lower Division, the four Chinese competitions of nation China, French CFA, French Champions Trophy, Indian Dehli Senior Division.
- Not held: see the next section.

Austrian First Division: the 3.9.68 label 2000 row holds FC Austria Kärnten. That is the 2000/01 champion, so the 3.9.68 row is one year early. Part ger-aut-sui-lux shows the same shift for 1998 to 2000.

## Competitions not held at label 2001

- Afro-Asian Champions Cup: the 1999 edition was cancelled and CAF ended the cup in July 2000.
- CONCACAF Champions Cup: no 2001 edition.
- European Cup Winners Cup: last edition 1998/99.
- FIFA Club World Championship: the 2001 edition was cancelled in May 2001.
- Far East Club Championship: only edition 1998.
- Inter-American Cup: last edition 1998.
- South American CONMEBOL Cup: last edition 1999.
- South American Recopa: no Recopa from 1999 to 2002.
- Belgian League Cup: last final 2000 (1999/2000).
- Belgian Third Division: the tier was split into Third Division A and B. The notes give the A and B champions.
- Croatian Second Division: split into North and South groups from 2001/02.
- Croatian Super Cup: not played from 1995 to 2001.
- English Conference Cup: axed at the end of 2000/01. Label 2001 is 2001/02 under the season start convention.
- German Regional Division East and West/Southwest: both groups ended in 2000.
- Hong Kong Viceroy Cup: last edition 1998.

## Points of doubt

1. French CFA 2000/01. The page names no amateur champion in its notes for this year. In the palmarès table it highlights US Lusitanos Saint-Maur (group D winner). The same highlight marks the amateur champion in the other years where that club won its group. So `first` = Lusitanos de Saint-Maur is an inference.
2. Chinese First Division B, nation China. The 2001 champion is Shanghai Zhongyuan Huili. This club is not in the China databases. Part asia-1 maps its later name (Shanghai International) to Shaanxi Baorong Chanba FC, and I did the same (inference). The China PR row keeps Shanghai Zhongyuan.
3. Indian Dehli Senior Division. Label 2001 = the 2001-02 edition (Hindustan Club 1-0 Indian Nationals) follows the inference of part asia-2.
4. Brazilian Minas Gerais Lower Division. I matched Nacional de Uberaba to Nacional Futebol Clube (MG), the only Minas Gerais Nacional in the data (inference).
5. Argentine Second Division. `second` is Quilmes, the Apertura runner-up, as in the 3.9.68 row and the rule of part americas. Arsenal won the second promotion place.
6. Indian Haryana State Championship. I found no source. All fields are `unknown`.

## The `ended` column

`ended` is the month of the final or of the last league match day. Some label 2001 editions ended in 2002 or earlier than 2001:

- Chinese FA Super Cup: 2000-12 (the 2000 edition).
- Copa Mercosur: 2002-01 (final 24 January 2002).
- Argentine Premier Division Apertura 2001: 2002-02. The Wikipedia infobox gives 17 August 2001 to 5 February 2002.
- Argentine Premier Division Clausura, Argentine Second Division, Australian National Soccer League and all Croatian rows: 2002-05.
- Greek Cup and Greek National B Division: 2002-04.

These months are inferences:

- Copa Centro-Oeste and Copa Norte: RSSSF gives one date for the two final legs (17 March and 11 March 2001).
- English Conference: the page shows a league match on 5 May 2001 and no later date.
- Argentine Second Division: the last dated match is 18 May 2002. The play-off against Primera clubs has no date.
- Brazilian Rio de Janeiro Lower Division: 2001-10 is the end of the phase that decided the champion. A later qualifying tournament for the 2002 first level ran to December 2001. I did not count it.

`ended = unknown` (6 rows): Czech Second Division, Danish First Division, French CFA, Brazilian Santa Catarina State Championship, Indian Dehli Senior Division, Indian Haryana State Championship. I found no page with the date of the last match.

## Names not matched

None. Every `first` and `second` is a full name in a `clubs/*.tsv` file.
