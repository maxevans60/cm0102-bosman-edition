The Wikipedia table has 2. Vitória de Setúbal, 3. Varzim and 4. Maia, all three on 64 points (19 wins, 7 draws, 8 losses), in the order of goal difference (+23, +19, +17). Both sources have Varzim and Vitória on the same points, so the order depends on the tie-break. Portuguese rules of that time used head-to-head results first (inference, I did not find the rule on a page). RSSSF gives a reason for its order, and Wikipedia gives a points total for Maia that differs from RSSSF. I recommend that the row keeps Varzim, but the lead must decide.
