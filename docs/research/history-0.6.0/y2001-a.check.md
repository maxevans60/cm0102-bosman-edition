## Points for the research agent

1. Brazilian Santa Catarina State Championship has `ended = unknown`. RSSSF Brasil (`tablessz/sc2001.htm`) gives the final legs on May 30 and Jun 3, so the value can be `2001-06`. The row has only one source for this date.
2. German League Cup: the row note gives the final as 17 July 2001. RSSSF and Wikipedia both give 21 July 2001 in Mannheim. The month is the same, so `ended` is correct. Only the note is wrong.
3. French CFA 2000/01: RSSSF lists two fourth level champions for this season, Olympique Lyonnais (reserve team) and US Lusitanos de Saint-Maur. The row's reading (best amateur club = Lusitanos) agrees with RSSSF. A reader who expects the overall CFA champion would get the Lyon reserve team.
4. Finnish First Division: the Wikipedia list gives FC Hämeenlinna as champion and FF Jaro as also promoted. It does not show the play-off final, so `second = FF Jaro` agrees by promotion only.
5. Belgian Third Division A and B: Dutch Wikipedia says the season ended on 26 May 2001. The row note gives 6 May. Both are in May, so `ended` agrees.
