# Xbox CM 02/03 database as a 2002/03 source (research, 2026-10-24)

Question: can the data of the Xbox game "Championship Manager - Season 02-03" serve as the reference for a 2002/03 season database? Max's direction (2026-10-24): use it as a research source in the own-database process (`tools/seasons/own_db`), the same way as the FM2005 and FM2007 facts. No separate converter.

Source: Max's own disc image (Europe, En/De/It, 7.8 GB). It was read only, with `python3 -I`. Scripts are in `/Users/max/Projects/cm0102/xbox-0203-scripts/`. The copied data files are in `/Users/max/Projects/cm0102/xbox-0203-work/disc/`. Both folders are outside the repo. Commit no Xbox data and nothing made from it.

## 1. Disc layout

- The image is a standard Xbox XISO (header at 0x10000). It holds 738 files, 1.16 GB in total.
- Top level: `default.xbe`, `environment.ini`, `Formations/` (43 tactics), `qstarts/` (54 quick starts, one per nation in English and German) and `data/`.
- `data/` has the same file set as the PC `Data` folder: `index.dat`, `staff.dat`, `club.dat`, `nat_club.dat`, `nation.dat`, the three name files, the history files, `city.dat`, `stadium.dat`, `officials.dat`, `euro.cfg`, `player_setup.cfg` and the `.lng` and `.ldb` language files.
- The executable reports version string 3.9.63. The game is a port of the CM 01/02 engine.

## 2. Record layouts

The Xbox files use the PC 3.9.68 layouts with no change. Both store the staff table in format 1 (index version 1, 157 bytes per row in the file). The Update Team databases store format 2 (version 2, 110 bytes per row), which is the layout that `tools/seasons/own_db/cmdb.py` reads. cmdb.py refuses format 1 on purpose. A reader for this source must read format 1.

Format 1 staff row, found in the data (2026-10-24): names at 4, 8 and 12, date of birth 0x10, nation 0x1a, club 0x39, joined 0x3e, contract end 0x46, flags 0x5f (bit 2 player, bit 1 non-player), player row id 0x91 (table 10, 70 bytes), non-player row id 0x99 (table 9, 68 bytes). Proof: 0x91 covers all 139,769 rows of table 10 once, and the linked rows of Henry, Zidane, Owen, Figo, Raúl and Beckham hold current abilities of 177 to 190.

## 3. What the Xbox data holds

People were matched by first name, surname and date of birth (`diff_staff.py`).

- The Xbox database is a cut-down copy of the same SI data set as 3.9.68. It keeps 74,485 people. 74,025 of them are also in 3.9.68. Only 26 are new.
- It drops people at about 3,800 small clubs. Clubs in the playable leagues keep their squads (Manchester United 69 and 69, Juventus 64 and 64, SK Brann 58 and 58).
- 72,593 matched people (98%) are at the same club in both. 13 of those have another contract end date.
- 1,432 matched people are at another club. 492 of them move between two clubs. 711 have a club on Xbox and none in 3.9.68. 229 have a club in 3.9.68 and none on Xbox. Some of these rows can be two people with the same name and an unknown date of birth. Each row needs a check before use.
- Examples of moves: Fabio Celestini to Olympique Marseille, Vitaly Kutuzov to Sporting, Stephen Lovell to Dundee United, many moves in the lower French and Portuguese divisions. Most have a join date of July or August 2002.
- Club names: "Fiorentina" is "Florentia Viola" on Xbox (the club was refounded in August 2002).
- Competitions: the Turkish 2. Division has 3 groups ("Category B Group A to C") on Xbox and 5 groups in 3.9.68.
- History: both end with 2002 as the last season. The rows differ in club ids. I did not compare the winners row by row.

## 4. Main finding

The PC 3.9.68 data already holds the summer 2002 transfers. Both data sets have about 9,000 joins dated July to September 2002. Examples in 3.9.68: Ronaldo at Real Madrid (joined August 2002), Rio Ferdinand at Manchester United (July 2002), Robbie Keane at Tottenham (August 2002). The Reddit point that 3.9.68 stops in March 2002 does not agree with the files.

The game already has a 2002/03 tile that starts July 2002 with the 3.9.68 data (Q233, spec 2026-10-16 seasons). So the Xbox source adds about 500 to 1,000 club changes, one club name and one Turkish group change on top of what the 2002/03 tile already has.

## 6. Gaps

- The Xbox data has 74,485 people. People it lacks keep their 3.9.68 record. The Xbox data cannot say that they left a club.
- Rows where a person is at a club in 3.9.68 and free on Xbox (229) can be a real release or a person that the cut-down copy dropped from that club. The process must not release a person on that evidence alone.
- The Turkish group change is a competition structure change. It needs game code, and it is outside a data-only database.
- There is no later SI data set to check the Xbox moves against. A check against public sources is possible for the playable leagues only.

## 7. Decisions so far (Max, 2026-10-24)

- Q1: build it. Q2: 3.9.68 is the template, with checked ratings for major players. Q3: matched people take the club, join date and contract end, and major players take the Xbox rating changes.
- Q4: a person who is free on Xbox becomes free. Q5: rename Fiorentina to Florentia Viola and make the Turkish 3-group change.
- Q6 and Q7: a change list comes first. The database ships as a standalone zip and replaces the plain 3.9.68 data on the 2002/03 tile.
- Open: Q8 to Q13 (major player rule, rating source, release guard, Turkish code slice, name and dates, credit).
