Date 2026-10-19. Code: `src/recomp_rt/win/season_club_cups.c`. Test: `tests/seasons/club_cups_check.py`. The rules table: `rules-0.6.0/club-world.tsv`, rows `sudamericana`, `recopa`, `suruga_bank`, `fifa_intercontinental_cup`, `acl_two`. The module: `season_comps.h` and `2026-10-18-new-comps.md`. ## 1. One class for five cups

All five are objects of the World_Club_Cup class, the class of the original's Intercontinental Cup.

A Cup holds any club count and lets clubs enter in a later round (`map_fixture_tree` 0x519265..0x519504, formats doc 3.2). So each cup is a knockout of the table's club count, with the byes in the first round when the count is not a power of two. The wraps of 0x92b8b0 and 0x92b770 test the object's record (+4): a def's record gets the def's clubs and rounds, every other object the original's. The original's Intercontinental Cup keeps its record, so it is unchanged.

A two-legged round copies the Libertadores fields: +0x0d = 4, +0x0f = 1, +0x21 = 2 legs, +0x22 days to the second leg (0x4c343e..0x4c346a).

Each def object gets a copy of the vtable (`season_comps_vtable`) with slot 5 replaced: on the day before the first draw it runs the picker again into the same club list, then jumps to `Cup::update_for_date` 0x51f890. The original writes into a made club list the same way (the Libertadores winner into the Intercontinental Cup, 0x4c41cc, 0x4c41d0).

## 3. Years and dates

The Sudamericana, the Recopa and the Suruga Bank match play one edition a calendar year, and +0x40 is that year. The edition of year Y takes the row part (parts are separated by `||`, club counts by `;`) whose first date lies in Y, from the row of season Y or Y - 1. So the two editions in 2016/17 (the Recopa and the Sudamericana of 2016 and 2017) come from the dates. The Intercontinental Cup and ACL Two play one edition a season (ACL Two: its dates start after its qualifying clause).

The dates of a part: every day with its own month, or joined by "and", "-" or an en dash to the next day, which gives the month and year ("24 and 31 August 2005", "17 September 2024 – 18 May 2025"). The first match is on the first date, the final (its first leg) on the last date, the rounds between evenly. A round's draw is 7 days before its first match, or the day after the round before.

With changing rules off the start season's edition stays, moved to each later year (Q232).

A year with no edition: the before hook sets +0x40 to the year of the next edition - 1 (start_new_season adds 1), or calls `season_change_skip()` for a cup that never returns (the Suruga Bank match after 2019, `season_comps.h`). An edition must end before the next update day of its entry, or the object is not complete and start_new_season does not run (0x5d5ccb).

## 4. Clubs

The history: rows of 0x1a bytes at [0xae23d4], count [0xae237c]: +4 the record, +8 the game year when written, +0xa winner, +0xe runner-up, +0x12 third (`comp_util_add_history_record` 0x4afce0, 0x4afd51..0x4afd7b). A picker reads the row of a record and a year.

| Cup | Clubs |
|---|---|
| Copa Sudamericana | Marked clubs (club +0x1db = the record's id, +0x1df = 1). The marks come from a wrap of `assign_libertadores_places` 0x4c6430: after the original, while the seeding's sorted list lives (freed at 0x4c6267), `pick_club_by_position` 0x4c6730 (list, count, nation id, 1, 1, 0) gives the next club of a nation with no place, as the Libertadores places do (0x4c66bc..0x4c66fc). Argentina and Brazil take the places of the row ("ARG 6", "BRA 8"), the other nations of the seeding rows (10 bytes each, +0 nation id, count +0x6e, 0x4c6154) share the rest; Mexico takes none |
| Recopa | the Libertadores winner (key id 0x9cf63c) and the Sudamericana winner of Y - 1 |
| Suruga Bank | the J.League Cup winner (key id 0x9cf664, record "Japanese Cup") and the Sudamericana winner of Y - 1 |
| FIFA Intercontinental Cup | in club list order OFC (0x9cf7cc), AFC (0x9cf64c), CAF, CONMEBOL (0x9cf63c), CONCACAF (0x9cf72c), UEFA (0x9cf6e8): the winners of year Y |
| ACL Two | 32 Asian clubs of the top reputations |

The seeding runs on 10 January and at the new game (`update_season` 0x4c61b0 from the Libertadores constructor 0x4c2fe9 and its start_new_season 0x4c3a03). `collect_clubs` 0x4c6280 sets +0x1db = -1 for every club of a CONMEBOL nation or Mexico first (0x4c6377), so last year's marks go.

A club that the history or the marks cannot give comes from `WorldClubChamp::pick_entrant` 0x92ae60 (thiscall (continent, nation, picked, count, record), `ret 0x14`): with the record's history the winner or runner-up, else a club of the continent's top reputations (formats doc 5.2). Inference: it reads only +0x40 of its object (0x92af4e), so the cup object can be its `this`.

The Intercontinental Cup stepladder (club list OFC, AFC, CAF, CONMEBOL, CONCACAF, UEFA): round 0 the OFC v AFC play-off; round 1 two ties with CAF, CONMEBOL and CONCACAF new from index 2 (the play-off winner takes the empty slot); round 2 the Challenger Cup; round 3 the final with UEFA new (index 5).

## 5. Step L1: the Libertadores places by nation

`Conmebol_Seeding` [0xae1000] holds up to 11 rows of 10 bytes: +0 the nation id, +8 the places (a nation enters when it is Mexico or of continent 0x9cfa1c, 0x4c6101..0x4c611c). The original writes the places once, at its first build (Brazil and Argentina 4, Venezuela and Mexico 1, others 3: 0x4c60f4..0x4c6176, sum 31), and then reads them from its save. `assign_libertadores_places` places the holder first (0x4c6458), then fills each nation's count from the league order (0x4c66b7..0x4c6704). `collect_entrants` 0x4c3f40 takes exactly 32 clubs; another count shows the error box (0x4c3fe2).

The real group stage has 32 clubs from 2004/05 on: the table's 36, 38, 40 and 47 count the qualifying clubs too. So the wrap of 0x4c6430 writes byte +8 of each row before the original, from the places of the Libertadores row: the edition of year Y starts in January or February Y, so its row is season Y - 1 (Q276). "ARG 6+1, BRA 7+1": 6 and 7, the "+1" is the holder. Mexico keeps 1 place to 2015/16, then none. The other nations share the rest of 31 in row order. After the original, a nation short of clubs leaves free places, and the next clubs of the other nations take them, so the count is 32.

## 5b. The CONCACAF Champions Cup's club count

`Con_Champ` (constructor 0x4c0c40, vtable 0x968aa0, Cup base, entry 4) plays 8 clubs in 3 one-match rounds: round table 0x4c0f00 for stage -1 only (0x4c0f0a), draw 1 August, matches 28 September, 1 and 4 October. The constructor sets +0x40 (0x4c0c8d), then the club list of 8 (calloc count 0x4c0cc2, +0x3e at 0x4c0cc4). The picker 0x4c11a0 sets +0x3e = 8 again (0x4c11af) and writes 8 fixed slots: Mexico, the MLS and US Open Cup winners, Central America, the Caribbean (0x4c1203..0x4c136b); it returns 1 (0x4c1433). `start_new_season` 0x4c1440 makes a list of +0x3e clubs (0x4c14de..0x4c14f5) before it adds 1 to +0x40, and its slot 35 makes the round table again.

In a season game the three count sites take the table's count of the edition's season (`tools/recomp/seasons.py`, `rt_concacaf_clubs`): 16 (2001/02, 2002/03, 2017/18 to 2022/23), 8 (2003/04 to 2007/08), 24 (2008/09 to 2016/17), 27 (2023/24 on). The before hook sets +0x3e for the next edition. The round table wrap gives a knockout with the byes in the first round (`plan_knockout`). The picker wrap keeps the original's 8 clubs at the end of the list, where the byes enter, and fills the first places with the North American clubs of the highest reputation. The base game and an 8-club season keep the original's code path.

## 6. Decisions (recommended answers, for Max to confirm)

- Q-26c-14 (CONCACAF). One match a round, 3 days apart, in the original's weeks (from 28 September), not the table's two legs and spring dates. The group seasons (2008/09 to 2016/17) play as a knockout of the same count (a Cup holds no groups: +0x2c = 0, 0x4c0c9b). 15 clubs (2021/22) play as 16. The added clubs are the North American clubs of the highest reputation, not the table's places by nation (Q333 needs the MLS table).

- Q-26c-12 (L1). The Libertadores keeps 32 group clubs. The table's qualifying clubs (the counts above 32) do not play: a qualifying stage needs a new object (counts research, step L2).
- Q-26c-13. Libertadores places of the nations other than Argentina and Brazil: the rest of 31 shared equally in row order, Mexico 1 place to 2015/16 and none after.


- Q-26c-7. Each new cup is a knockout of the table's club count. Not built: the national sections of the Sudamericana (2003 to 2008), its group stage from 2021, the ten or twelve Libertadores clubs that join it from 2017, the groups of ACL Two, the points rule of the Recopa (2005 to 2013: two legs here, as in a knockout).
- Q-26c-8. Sudamericana places of nations other than Argentina and Brazil: the rest of the table's count, shared out.
- Q-26c-9. ACL Two clubs: 32 Asian clubs of the top reputations. A club can play the AFC Champions League and ACL Two in one season.
- Q-26c-10. The Intercontinental Cup has no African club competition to read: a club of Africa's top reputations.
- Q-26c-11. The round of the stepladder that the play-off winner meets: the empty slot that `map_fixture_tree` gives it (CAF or the Derby tie, not read).
