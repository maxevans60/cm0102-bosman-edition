## Hosts

A host row of `HostCountry` holds six nation fields (+6 to +0x1a, three pairs). `find_hosts` 0x5fa730 reads pair 1 only for a fixed row (choice 0xfd: 0x5fa768, 0x5fa7a0..0x5fa7a9), and `find_next_hosts` 0x5fa7d0 the same (0x5fa7ef, 0x5fa81c). Slice 27 writes fixed rows. So `row_write` of `season_intl.c` now writes up to six hosts of the table in order into the six fields, and `season_intl_host_ids` reads them. The original's code still sees two hosts. The save writes every row (0x5fb3ce), so a load keeps them. The editions of slice 27 with more than two hosts (Asian Cup 2007, ACN 2027) change in no value that the original reads.

Host news: a fixed row gets no news from `daily_update` 0x5fa850. With changing rules on, the season change of the edition's season posts "The 2026 World Cup plays in Canada, Mexico and United States, its 3 host nations." (`host_news` in `season_intl.c`). A game that starts in that season gets no news (a start season gives none).
