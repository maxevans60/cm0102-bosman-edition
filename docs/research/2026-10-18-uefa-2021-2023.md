## The table

`make_uefa_table.py` now reads 2001/02 to 2023/24 (`LAST` 2023) and gives each row:

- `uc_ko_po`: the clubs of the knockout round play-offs, from the round "KO play-offs 16" of the rounds list. The group text "winners to the round of 16; runners-up play the Champions League group thirds in knockout round play-offs" gives two clubs through a group.
- `away_goals`: 0 when the Champions League format says "no away goals rule".
- `suspended`: the nations of the access list row with "<nation> suspended: no places" in its notes, separated by commas (Russia from 2022/23).
- `ecl_rounds`, `ecl_groups`, `ecl_group_clubs`, `ecl_ko_po`: the Conference League row (comp_file `new`, comp `Conference League`), none when not held. Part b reads them.
- `DROPS` from 2021/22: Champions Path and League Path Q2 losers to the Europa League Q3, Champions Path Q3 losers to the play-offs, League Path Q3 losers and play-off losers to the groups. The Europa League rows count "21 entered + 37 from the Champions League" in 2021/22: 10 + 3 + 6 + 4 + 6 and the 8 thirds. The preliminary round and Q1 losers go to the Conference League (part b).

The access list from 2021/22 has three competitions. The tool reads `ecl` (code `k`) and `ecl-holder` (`K`, or `Y` for its Europa League place), and the league cup note of the Conference League rows (`M`, apart from the Europa League's `L`). ## The Europa League knockout round play-offs (season_uefa_cup.c)

The group stage is slice 23's (8 groups of 4, match days of 2009/10). After the groups the object holds a Cup_Stage of 24 clubs in stage 8:

- List slots 0 to 15: 8 ties, each a Champions League third (the slots count - 8 to count - 1 of the entry table, the original's hand-over at 0x586282) against a group runner-up of another nation when a draw is found. The runner-up plays the second leg at home (the first club of a pair is the home side of the first leg, as slice 22's round of 32).
- List slots 16 to 23: the 8 group winners.
- The thirds and fourths of the groups are out (+0x1db = -1, as 0x5862fe). Part b sends the thirds to the Conference League.
- The round table: round 0 (code 30) the 16 clubs of slots 0 to 15 in fixed ties (+0xd 0x200: the draw 0x51c410 keeps the pairs, 0x51c5e4). Round 1 (code 40, round of 16) takes the 8 winners and 8 new clubs from slot 16 (+0x1c 8, +0x1e 16), a seeded draw (+0xd 0x400) that avoids two clubs of one nation (+0xb 4). Then the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final of slice 22. The dates of slice 22's round of 32 and round of 16.
- The seeded draw 0x51d2d0 pairs the clubs of the higher tier (entry byte +4) with the others. The Cup_Stage constructor 0x522e00 writes +4 from its fifteenth argument, a byte array, when that is not 0 (0x522fa6..0x522fc6; else the slot index when the thirteenth is not 0, else 0). The runtime passes 1 for slots 0 to 15 and 2 for the group winners, so each winner meets a play-off winner.

The rules report counts 24 clubs in that knockout stage. `rules_report_check.py` (kind `format`) reads "KO play-offs 16, KO 16" as 16 + 16 - 8 = 24.

A small fix of slice 23's code: the round table wrap chose the access list format by "groups != 8" (to keep slice 22's 8 groups of 5 apart). From 2021/22 the Europa League has 8 groups again, so the wrap now asks whether the season has an access list.

## No away goals rule (season_uefa.c)

The evidence:

- The tie result code of the Cup tests flag 0x4 of its flags argument at 0x51f751 (`test bl, al` with bl 4). With 0x4 clear and the tie level, it compares the away goals (0x51f762..0x51f79a) and writes winner code 6 (+0x3d or +0x3e). With 0x4 set it jumps past that to 0x51f79e. Flag 0x4000 (away goals only after extra time) is tested next at 0x51f755.
- The match engine 0x6bd930 tests the same flag before it compares the away goals (0x6bdab9 `test dl, 4`, 0x6bdbc1 `and eax, 4`). So with 0x4 a level aggregate goes to extra time (flag 0x2) and penalties (0x1).
- The second leg takes the flags of the round's +0x0f, OR'd in after the first leg (`2026-10-17-cup-round-record.md`, section 3, 0x51d12f). Slice 21a uses the same bit for the League Cup semi-finals (`season_cups.c`, type `N`).

`season_uefa_no_away_goals(table, rounds, season)` sets 0x4 in +0x0f of each two-legged round (+0x21 = 2) of a round table when the row of the season has `away_goals` 0. It is called for the four round tables the runtime makes: the Champions League qualifying rounds (`season_uefa_cl_qual_table`), its knockout (`ko_table` of season_uefa.c), the Europa League qualifying rounds (`season_uefa_uc_qual_table`) and its knockout (`ko_table` of season_uefa_cup.c). The finals are one match (+0x21 1) and keep the original's 0x83 (golden goal flag 0x80 too: the original's, not changed here). Each call writes a log line "no away goals rule in n of m rounds".

## Russia (season_uefa_seeding.c)

From 2022/23 the row names Russia as suspended (Q293; Q365 keeps it so to 2027/28, slice 25). The published access lists of 2022/23 and 2023/24 remove the Russian places and give them to other associations (the research section "Slice 24"). In the game:

- The nation's record id is read from its key global (the nations table of slice 17, `season_nation.h`, "Russia" 0x9cf448), as `season_kind_nation.c` reads it.
- Russia's entry of the seeding gets no Champions League and no Europa League place (entry +0xc and +0xd 0, so the league table shows no European places). A Russian title holder gets no holder place.
- The cascade (step 6 of slice 23) fills the free places with the clubs of the rounds below and the best-ranked other nations. It takes no filler from a suspended nation.
- In 2021/22 Russia keeps its places: the suspension came in February 2022, during the season (Q278).
- A log line "suspended Russia: n clubs with a place" counts, from the club table, the clubs of a suspended nation with a Champions League or Europa League mark (+0x1db). The test wants 0.

The national team is not in this slice (slices 27 and 28).

## Decisions for Max (recommended answers taken)

1. **Free places (Russia's and the title holders').** The game gives them through the cascade of slice 23, with the real fallback of 2021/22 on for the Europa League: the best-ranked cup winners of the round below move up one round, and the gap at the bottom (Q3) goes to the cup winner of the best-ranked association with no Europa League place (`el_cup_filler`). This gives the published moves: 2021/22 Russia (7) to the groups, Denmark (13) to the play-offs, Cyprus (16) to Q3; 2022/23, with Russia removed, Turkey (13) and Denmark (14) to the play-offs, Serbia (16) and Czech Republic (17) to Q3. The Champions League places of Russia go the same way as slice 23's cascade: the champions and runners-up of the next ranks move up. Inference: the clubs then match the published lists where the game's association ranks match the real ones. The game ranks associations by its own coefficient (0x903b50), so a rank can differ.
   Rule `Y` (the Conference League title holder, a Europa League group place, 2022/23 on): with part b the holder is the history winner of the Conference League record. With no winner (a game that starts in 2022/23 or later has no Conference League history) the place is filled by the same fallback as a holder that qualified through its league, which is what happened in 2022/23 (Roma).
2. **The round of 16 draw.** A seeded draw: each group winner meets a play-off winner, with no two clubs of one nation when a draw is found. The real draw also keeps clubs of one nation apart, the same.
3. **The 2023 Super Cup.** Lead decision: penalties after 90 minutes, as the row says. The row field `sc_extra_time` is 0 from 2023. The original writes the round flags 0x83 at 0x57a4be (extra time 0x2, golden goal 0x80, penalties 0x1). The wrap of 0x57a3c0 keeps only 0x1.
4. **Part b: one path a Conference League round.** As slice 23 did for the Europa League: the Champions Path and main path of a round are one round, with the real club count. Recommended, because a second path doubles the ties code for no change a player sees in the counts. All Champions League Q1 losers enter Q2 (one real loser went to Q3).
5. **Part b: the record's level.** The new record takes the UEFA Cup's level 18 (+0x69). Its meaning is not known (slice 26's module calls it the level, an inference). Recommended: the same as the cup it is built like.
6. **Part b: the Europa League thirds.** The Conference League takes them from the Europa League group tables when it draws its knockout round play-offs, not through a hand-over of the Europa League. Recommended: one place in the code reads them, whichever cup updates first.
7. **The expected rules report files of 2021 to 2023** are made once, on the build with part b (part b changes those reports), not on the part a build as the state file planned.

- Def `conference_league`: comp `Conference League` (rows uefa-6 to uefa-206, comp_file `new`), file `new_conference_league`, record "UEFA Europa Conference League" (a new record: `like` "UEFA Cup", short name "Conference League", level 18 as the UEFA Cup's, 0x79d447), entry 3 (Europe).
- `ecl_make`: operator new 0x944cf1 with 0xb3 bytes (as 0x8314bf), then `Uefa_Cup::Uefa_Cup` 0x9022e0 (thiscall: year, record; ret 8) through `rt_callback` (`tools/recomp/callbacks.py`). No vtable copy: the constructor writes the vtable 0x970790 at 0x90232d.
- The module makes the object in the entry hook of the first season that the table holds it (2021/22), renames the record to the name of the season (rows: "UEFA Europa Conference League" to 2023/24, "UEFA Conference League" from 2024/25) and sends the news "The UEFA Europa Conference League is played for the first time." At a load it makes the object again from its saved file.
- `season_comps_file`: the file of the def whose record an object has. `season_change_file` and the rules report ask it first, so the object is `new_conference_league` everywhere and not `uefa_cup` (else the names kind would give its record the Europa League's name).

### Two objects of one class (season_uefa_cup.c, season_uefa_qual.c)

`season_uefa_cup_kind` tells them apart by record: 'e' when the record id is the key id [0x9cf6f0], 'k' when the record's data name is the Conference League's. The record pointer +4 is set at 0x90231d, before the constructor's first call, so the kind holds in the constructor too. Each wrap that tested the vtable now tests the kind: the round table 0x9025c0, build_entrants 0x902b90, the stage array at a load 0x51fc00, the stage step 0x5a8f60, the honours 0x519a90 and slot 17 0x9030e0 act on both with the row of the kind (`uc_format`: the `ecl_*` fields); the format of 2004/05 to 2008/09 and the Intertoto hand-over stay the Europa League's.

### Places (season_uefa_seeding.c)

After the Europa League places, each nation gets its Conference League places of the `k` rows: the first is the cup winner when the nation has no Europa League place, the last is the league cup winner for the `M` rows (England), the others the first clubs with no place. The title holder (the history winner of the new record, 0x4afd80) gets a Europa League group place from 2022/23 (`Y` rows) or a Conference League place (`K`). Then the cascade of slice 23 runs for the Conference League on one path (codes 21 to 25). A club with a place gets the record id of the Conference League at +0x1db.

### Rounds and drops

- The rounds have one path in the game, as the Europa League's: Q1, Q2, Q3 and the play-offs (codes 250, 260, 270, 220; `ECL_LEVELS`, on the Europa League's days), then 8 groups of 4, knockout round play-offs and a round of 16 (`draw_knockout_po`), as the Europa League.
- The row field `ecl_drops` ("c:PR>Q2 c:Q1>Q2 e:Q3>PO e:PO>G", from the club counts: "136 entered + 45" in 2021/22 = 19 + 8 + 10 and the 8 thirds) gives each round slots for the losers of the other cups. The Champions League losers come as they come to the Europa League (`cl_losers`, all ties of a round at once). A Europa League loser is a side of a tie of its round with entry byte +5 = -1 (0x51b5cd) that is in no tie of the next round, so it comes as soon as its tie ends, in the slot of its tie. In the last qualifying round the original marks the winner with +5 = 1 (0x51af97), so there the loser is the other side. The Europa League's round table and ties stay until its start_new_season frees them (0x902ee0), so its play-off losers are read after it draws its groups too. A group draw waits until it has its 32 clubs (slice 23).
- The knockout round play-offs take the 8 Europa League group thirds: the third row of each Europa League group table (League::get_table_row 0x6826d0) goes into the last 8 slots, as the Champions League thirds go to the Europa League (0x586282). Both group stages end on the same match day, so the tables are final whichever cup draws first (in a 2021/22 game the Conference League drew its groups before the Europa League did). The Europa League's own draw keeps the id of a third that the Conference League took.

### Names and the report

- Round names (`docs/research/round-names.tsv`, file `new_conference_league`): Q1 to Q3, the play-off round, the knockout round play-offs and the round of 16, from 2021/22. `make_round_names.py` takes a `new_` file of `season_comps.c` or a header.
- The uefa line of the rules report (`report_comp`) and its round labels for the Conference League. `season_uefa_report_clubs` counts its clubs. `rules_report_check.py` gives a row of comp_file `new` the file "new_" and its comp ("new_conference_league").

## Differences to the real competitions that stay

- The Conference League rounds have one path (as slice 23's Europa League). All Champions League first qualifying round losers enter its Q2; in the real draw one of them went to Q3. The counts of each round are the real ones.
- A Conference League club plays with the ruling body's rules and no European squad list (0x5832e0, 0x85a9b0 know the three cups of the original), and some domestic cups do not skip it (research doc B.3). Not a crash.
