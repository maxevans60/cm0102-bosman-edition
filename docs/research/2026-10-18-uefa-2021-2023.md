## The table

`make_uefa_table.py` now reads 2001/02 to 2023/24 (`LAST` 2023) and gives each row:

- `uc_ko_po`: the clubs of the knockout round play-offs, from the round "KO play-offs 16" of the rounds list. The group text "winners to the round of 16; runners-up play the Champions League group thirds in knockout round play-offs" gives two clubs through a group.
- `away_goals`: 0 when the Champions League format says "no away goals rule".
- `suspended`: the nations of the access list row with "<nation> suspended: no places" in its notes, separated by commas (Russia from 2022/23).
- `ecl_rounds`, `ecl_groups`, `ecl_group_clubs`, `ecl_ko_po`: the Conference League row (comp_file `new`, comp `Conference League`), none when not held. Part b reads them.
- `DROPS` from 2021/22: Champions Path and League Path Q2 losers to the Europa League Q3, Champions Path Q3 losers to the play-offs, League Path Q3 losers and play-off losers to the groups. The Europa League rows count "21 entered + 37 from the Champions League" in 2021/22: 10 + 3 + 6 + 4 + 6 and the 8 thirds. The preliminary round and Q1 losers go to the Conference League (part b).

The access list from 2021/22 has three competitions. The tool reads `ecl` (code `k`) and `ecl-holder` (`K`, or `Y` for its Europa League place), and the league cup note of the Conference League rows (`M`, apart from the Europa League's `L`). A range of ranks with "-" (no Europa League place for the associations ranked 16 and below) gives places "" (none). The single rank "-" of Liechtenstein, which has no league, is left out as before. ctest `season_uefa` checks the rows.

## The Europa League knockout round play-offs (season_uefa_cup.c)

The group stage is slice 23's (8 groups of 4, match days of 2009/10). After the groups the object holds a Cup_Stage of 24 clubs in stage 8:

- List slots 0 to 15: 8 ties, each a Champions League third (the slots count - 8 to count - 1 of the entry table, the original's hand-over at 0x586282) against a group runner-up of another nation when a draw is found. The runner-up plays the second leg at home (the first club of a pair is the home side of the first leg, as slice 22's round of 32).
- List slots 16 to 23: the 8 group winners.
- The thirds and fourths of the groups are out (+0x1db = -1, as 0x5862fe). Part b sends the thirds to the Conference League.
- The round table: round 0 (code 30) the 16 clubs of slots 0 to 15 in fixed ties (+0xd 0x200: the draw 0x51c410 keeps the pairs, 0x51c5e4). Round 1 (code 40, round of 16) takes the 8 winners and 8 new clubs from slot 16 (+0x1c 8, +0x1e 16), a seeded draw (+0xd 0x400) that avoids two clubs of one nation (+0xb 4). Then the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final of slice 22. The dates of slice 22's round of 32 and round of 16.
- The seeded draw 0x51d2d0 pairs the clubs of the higher tier (entry byte +4) with the others. The Cup_Stage constructor 0x522e00 writes +4 from its fifteenth argument, a byte array, when that is not 0 (0x522fa6..0x522fc6; else the slot index when the thirteenth is not 0, else 0). The runtime passes 1 for slots 0 to 15 and 2 for the group winners, so each winner meets a play-off winner.

The rules report counts 24 clubs in that knockout stage. `rules_report_check.py` (kind `format`) reads "KO play-offs 16, KO 16" as 16 + 16 - 8 = 24.

A small fix of slice 23's code: the round table wrap chose the access list format by "groups != 8" (to keep slice 22's 8 groups of 5 apart). From 2021/22 the Europa League has 8 groups again, so the wrap now asks whether the season has an access list.

## No away goals rule (season_uefa.c)

The evidence:

- The tie result code of the Cup tests flag 0x4 of its flags argument at 0x51f751 (`test bl, al` with bl 4). With 0x4 clear and the tie level, it compares the away goals (0x51f762..0x51f79a) and writes winner code 6 (+0x3d or +0x3e). With 0x4 set it jumps past that to 0x51f79e. Flag 0x4000 (away goals only after extra time) is tested next at 0x51f755.
- The match engine 0x6bd930 tests the same flag before it compares the away goals (0x6bdab9 `test dl, 4`, 0x6bdbc1 `and eax, 4`). So with 0x4 a level aggregate goes to extra time (flag 0x2) and penalties (0x1).
- The second leg takes the flags of the round's +0x0f, OR'd in after the first leg (`2026-10-17-cup-round-record.md`, section 3, 0x51d12f). Slice 21a uses the same bit for the League Cup semi-finals (`season_cups.c`, type `N`).

`season_uefa_no_away_goals(table, rounds, season)` sets 0x4 in +0x0f of each two-legged round (+0x21 = 2) of a round table when the row of the season has `away_goals` 0. It is called for the four round tables the runtime makes: the Champions League qualifying rounds (`season_uefa_cl_qual_table`), its knockout (`ko_table` of season_uefa.c), the Europa League qualifying rounds (`season_uefa_uc_qual_table`) and its knockout (`ko_table` of season_uefa_cup.c). The finals are one match (+0x21 1) and keep the original's 0x83 (golden goal flag 0x80 too: the original's, not changed here). Each call writes a log line "no away goals rule in n of m rounds".

## Russia (season_uefa_seeding.c)

From 2022/23 the row names Russia as suspended (Q293; Q365 keeps it so to 2027/28, slice 25). The published access lists of 2022/23 and 2023/24 remove the Russian places and give them to other associations (the research section "Slice 24"). In the game:

- The nation's record id is read from its key global (the nations table of slice 17, `season_nation.h`, "Russia" 0x9cf448), as `season_kind_nation.c` reads it.
- Russia's entry of the seeding gets no Champions League and no Europa League place (entry +0xc and +0xd 0, so the league table shows no European places). A Russian title holder gets no holder place.
- The cascade (step 6 of slice 23) fills the free places with the clubs of the rounds below and the best-ranked other nations. It takes no filler from a suspended nation.
- In 2021/22 Russia keeps its places: the suspension came in February 2022, during the season (Q278).
- A log line "suspended Russia: n clubs with a place" counts, from the club table, the clubs of a suspended nation with a Champions League or Europa League mark (+0x1db). The test wants 0.

The national team is not in this slice (slices 27 and 28).

## Part b: the Conference League (not built)

Research: `docs/research/2026-10-18-conference-league-object.md`. Q364: a new competition record, so the Cup Winners' Cup record keeps its name and history. The findings that set the plan:

1. The record array is made inside `load_database` 0x528540, at a new game and at a load: club comps, nation comps, then 0x7f blank friendly records. The friendly code finds its records as count - 0x7f (0x5c210a, 0x5c29de). A new record must go between the nation comps and the friendly records, so nine constant sites inside the loader change (0x7f to 0x7f + K).
2. The save holds the array (`save_database` 0x52dad0 from `save_game` 0x82e6d6). A wrap of it sets the header count [0xae2344] to 0x7f + K, so a load reads K back. The base game keeps K = 0 (Q152). A save of an earlier build has K = 0, so its game never gets the Conference League.
3. A second object of the Uefa_Cup class (0xb3 bytes, constructor 0x9022e0) works with the original vtable. Only `build_entrants` 0x902b90 reads the key id 0x9cf6f0. Every wrap of slices 22 to 24 that tests the vtable 0x970790 must test the record id instead.
4. A fifth object goes into entry 3 with `season_change_add_comp`. No code reads that array by a fixed index.

This is "Open" 1 of slice 26's shared module (`season_comps.h`, new records). Part b builds it there, or in a small block of this slice when slice 26 is on main first. The table already holds the Conference League rounds, groups, play-offs and access list places (`ecl_*`, codes `k`, `K`, `Y`, `M`).
