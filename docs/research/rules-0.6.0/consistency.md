## 1. Stopped seasons

A league row of 2019/20 or 2020/21 now holds the places by the rules of that season in `up`, `down` and `playoffs`. What happened in the stopped, voided or curtailed season is in `notes`. Where no rule count was found, the value is `unknown`. Each value marked "inference" comes from the rule of the season before.

Rows left unchanged on purpose:

- aus-jap-kor-208 and aus-jap-kor-235 (J1 and J2 2020): the season was suspended, then played to the end. The J.League removed relegation on 19 March 2020. J1 2021 has 20 clubs because of that decision.
- rus-tur-181 and rus-tur-208 (Süper Lig and TFF 1. Lig 2019/20): suspended, then played to the end without relegation. Süper Lig 2020/21 has 21 clubs because of that decision.
- arg-usa-20 and arg-usa-47 (Argentina 2021): relegation was suspended before the season started, so 0 is the rule of that season.
- fra-175 (Championnat National 2020/21): played to the end. Only the last club went down because National 2 was void. The normal rule count was not found.

Note for Max: next-season club counts in the tables still follow the real outcome (for example Regionalliga 2020/21, Danish 2nd Division 2020/21 with 28 clubs, Belgian First Amateur Division 2020/21). A game that applies the rule places in 2019/20 will not reach those counts.

Rows that state only the FIFA framework now use one wording in every part:

- Seasons 2002/03 to 2022/23: "two registration periods: one between seasons (max 12 weeks) and one mid-season (max 4 weeks); exact dates: unknown". Source: RSTP January 2020 edition, article 6 paragraph 2, and the Transfer window page (compulsory from 2002/03).
- Seasons 2023/24 to 2027/28: "two registration periods (FIFA RSTP March 2023 edition, in force 1 April 2023): first 8 to 12 weeks between seasons, second 4 to 8 weeks mid-season, at most 16 weeks together; exact dates: unknown". Source: RSTP March 2023 edition, article 6 paragraph 2 and the in-force clause.

Rows with the federation's real dates or its own rule text are unchanged (for example eng, ger, ita, spa, fra, sco, den-swe, the Irish rows of wal-nir-irl, hol-bel-313). The Portuguese rows of 2023/24 to 2027/28 stated the old 12 plus 4 week limit, which was wrong for those seasons. The gre-pol-cro rows with their own federation names and dates are unchanged. Only the Greek rows up to 2022/23 lost the words "set by the association".
