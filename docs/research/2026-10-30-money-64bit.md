# Money above 2,147,483,647 (0.6.2 slice 11, faults 27 and 28)

The original keeps a club's bank balance as a signed 64-bit value (finance record +0x00, 0x167 bytes a club,
`FinanceTable::get_club_finance` 0x5a0590). Some readers keep only 32 bits. In a long game the richest club passes
2,147,483,647 in about 2055 and reaches about 12 billion by 2300 (gap 3 of the 0.6.2 audit: steady profit, no
inflation). This page lists every place found where a 64-bit money value is cut to 32 bits, what 0.6.2 does there,
and why the rest needs nothing. The code is `tools/recomp/original_faults.py` and `src/recomp_rt/win/original_faults.c`.
Every fix gives the original's result whenever the original's 32-bit value holds the right number, so it acts only
above 2,147,483,647 (Q152), except `get_max_wage` (below).

Method: a disassembly of every call of 0x5a0590 (163), `Finance::get_transfer_budget` 0x598800 (37), the finance
block getters 0x4671b0..0x4671e0 (32), and the reads of the manager totals +0xa4 and +0xac.

## The money text

- A Cash holds 32 bits: `Cash::Cash` 0x450a90 and `Cash::set` 0x4671a0 store one dword.
- `Cash::get_display_amount` 0x43f460 starts with `fild dword [ecx]`. After that the original works on 64 bits: the
  rounding step `cash_get_rounding_step` 0x4426e0 takes an int64 (`fild qword` at 0x4426ef), and the text function
  `cash_format_money` 0x441c10 takes an int64 (`Cash::format_display_amount` 0x450aa0 passes edx:eax at 0x450aaf).
- So only the first step is 32 bits. A screen that puts a 64-bit amount into a Cash now also gives the full amount to
  the runtime (`rt_cash_wide`), and 0x43f460 reads it from there while the Cash still holds its low word (two reads:
  the club info tab formats the balance twice, 0x4639a0 and 0x4639d5).

The screens fixed this way:

| Store | Function | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 0x46399c | `club_screen_draw_info_tab` 0x460590 | balance (high word at finance +4) |
| 0x463b28 | same | transfer budget |
| 0x808ef3 | `search_screens_build_player_search_screen` 0x808690 | transfer budget |
| 0x8ea2c0 | `transfer_screens_build_bid_screen` 0x8e9760 | transfer budget |
| 0x6b0366, 0x6b0399 | `manager_stats_screen_build` 0x6ae2a0 | manager money spent (+0xa4) and received (+0xac) |
| 0x887fa7, 0x88808f | `staff_screens_draw_history_tab` 0x885820 | the same two totals |

A test with balances of 2.5, 5 and 12 billion shows "£2.5B", "£5B" and "£12B" on Arsenal's Finances view
(`tests/seasons/original_faults_run.py money`).

## The transfer budget

`Finance::get_transfer_budget` 0x598800 (thiscall, finance record in ecx, ret 0x14) returns a 64-bit budget B in
edx:eax. Its steps: B starts as the balance (0x598807), takes the wage bill, stadium, reputation and nation terms in
64 bits (0x598b67..0x598f17), then:

1. Net spend N = this year's players bought (+0x110) less sold (+0x118), 64 bits. 0x598f4f re-extends only eax
   (`cdq`). Fixed: the high word stays (`rt_budget_hi`).
2. Q = pending fees + max(N - 8m, 0) (0x598f72..0x598f90). 0x598f8b and 0x598f92 re-extend only eax. Fixed the same
   way. B -= Q.
3. The floor R = balance - 10m (or 15m for one nation, 0x599185), kept as 32 bits in [esp+0x5c] (0x599135..0x59913d).
   Above it only a tenth of B counts: B = (B - R) x 0.1 + R (0x5991d0..0x59920e). With a balance above about
   2.157 billion, low32(balance) - 10m is negative, so a very rich club's budget fell to about a tenth. Fixed: the
   store keeps 2,147,483,647 and the runtime keeps the full R for the compare (0x5991d0, 0x5991d6) and the sum
   (0x599201).
4. The fog of war path cuts E + (B - E)/8 at 0x59979b. It needs B above about 17 billion, which no club reaches by
   2300 at the measured growth. Not changed.

The audit's "inner part" at 0x599024..0x599035 is not money: it is the club reputation squared times 0.5, left on the
x87 stack at 0x598df0.

Callers. 14 of the 37 calls keep the 64-bit result: 0x463afd and 0x463b20 (club info tab), 0x4d7541, 0x6970a3
(`__allmul`, `__alldiv`), 0x6a3b8c, 0x7b6d5f, 0x7b6e25, 0x808ee9 (player search), 0x8c0eb6, 0x8e74a7, 0x8e83a1,
0x8ea2b4 (bid screen), 0x8f4b28, 0x8f4bd5 (return addresses). The other 23 keep eax: a budget above 2,147,483,647 gave
them 0 or a wrong amount. The wrap of 0x598800 clamps the result to 2,147,483,647 for them. No fee or wage is that
large (fees are 32-bit), so the clamped budget buys anything a larger one would. The 23:

- AI: `Shortlist_Manager::allocate_transfer_budget` (0x83b74e, a negative eax gave 0: the club stopped buying),
  `try_add_player_to_club_shortlist` (0x84301b), `TRANSFER_OFFER::build_bid` (0x8e6335, fee = min(fee, budget)),
  `TRANSFER_MANAGER::check_offer_restrictions` (0x8cfe32), `handle_board_request` (0x6a8e48),
  `decide_ultimatum_response` (0x6acf9b).
- The human's bid screen: the fee colour, the fee cap and "fee = budget" (0x8ecf84, 0x8ed156, 0x8ed440, 0x8ed5a6,
  0x8ede34, 0x8ee025, 0x8ee3fa, 0x8ee622). The Offer Contract screens (0x4de3ab, 0x4de810): 0 to 5 million.
- News: the finance boost, cash injection, board takeover and job offer items (0x59e32e, 0x59e4e2, 0x59e7bd,
  0x59e907, 0x59eb1f, 0x59ed3d, 0x6a5021). A news item keeps its amounts in 32-bit slots (`NewsItem::set_slot`
  0x785790), so such a text shows at most £2,147m.

## The top wage

`Finance::get_max_wage` 0x5968d0 takes ftol(1.3 x balance) (`fild qword` 0x5968db, the factor at 0x968de8) and keeps
only eax (0x5968ee). At 0x597019 it adds a wage term in 32 bits and divides by 52; the result is taken from the wage
bill (0x59703a). The sum wraps once 1.3 x balance plus the term passes 2,147,483,647, from a balance of about
1.65 billion, and the club's top wage goes wrong. Fixed: the sum keeps the high word of the product and is clamped
to the 32-bit range (`rt_max_wage_sum`). This fix acts from about 1.65 billion, where the original's sum has already
wrapped, not from 2,147,483,647.

## Cuts that need no change

| Place | Why |
|---|---|
| Debt below -2,147,483,647: the club info tab's negative branch (0x463a0a), `try_board_takeover` (0x59eaa8..0x59ecfb) | A debt of 2.1 billion needs losses that no club in the soak saves comes near. I did not check the takeover and board payment rules (`try_board_takeover` 0x59e940, `try_board_debt_payment` 0x59dd40) that act on debt. |
| Players bought and sold in one period on the club info tab (0x462d28..0x46642c, 16 reads) | Season totals of 32-bit fees, reset each season (0x59cf19). The largest in the soak saves is 59.6 million. Not reached. |
| `comp_util_get_club_strength_score` 0x4c0720 (balance / 300 at 0x4c0782) | Wraps from about 644 billion. Not reached. |
| Fog path 0x59979b | About 17 billion (above). Not reached. |

64-bit reads that are right: `get_wealth_band` 0x598703, 0x69eb99, 0x7f9238, 0x6a9416, `handle_board_request`
(0x6a8abb, 0x6a8aeb, 0x6a8c3c, 0x6a8db7, 0x6a92ec), 0x6ad1f4, 0x7b6d6d, 0x7b6e3c, 0x8f4b36, 0x8f4bec,
`try_board_debt_payment` (0x59de6c, 0x59dea1), 0x598fc6, and the income and cost functions 0x5987c0..0x5999e0 (add/adc,
sub/sbb).

Not checked: finance reads that do not go through 0x5a0590; the 276 direct callers of 0x441c10 beyond the sites
above; how 0x441c10 writes amounts above 99 billion (12 billion shows as "£12B").
