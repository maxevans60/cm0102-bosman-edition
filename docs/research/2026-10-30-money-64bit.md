# Money above 2,147,483,647 (0.6.2 slice 11, faults 27 and 28)

The original keeps a club's bank balance as a signed 64-bit value (finance record +0x00, 0x167 bytes a club,
`FinanceTable::get_club_finance` 0x5a0590). Some readers keep only 32 bits. In a long game the richest club passes
2,147,483,647 in about 2055 and reaches about 12 billion by 2300 (gap 3 of the 0.6.2 audit: steady profit, no
inflation). This page lists every place found where a 64-bit money value is cut to 32 bits, what 0.6.2 does there,
and why the rest needs nothing. The code is `tools/recomp/original_faults.py` and `src/recomp_rt/win/original_faults.c`.
Every fix gives the original's result whenever the original's 32-bit value holds the right number, so it acts only
above 2,147,483,647 (Q152), except `get_max_wage` (below).

Method, from both ends:
- From the 64-bit values: every call of 0x5a0590 (163), `Finance::get_transfer_budget` 0x598800 (37), the finance
  block getters 0x4671b0..0x4671e0 (32), and the reads of the manager totals +0xa4 and +0xac.
- From the text: every money text goes through `cash_format_money` 0x441c10 (253 direct calls), and each of them
  takes its amount from `Cash::get_display_amount` 0x43f460, so every money text starts from a 32-bit Cash. The 372
  calls of 0x43f460 and the 92 of `Cash::format_display_amount` 0x450aa0 were each followed back to the amount and
  sorted: a 32-bit amount by nature (a fee, wage, price, gate, prize, fine or value), or one made from a 64-bit
  value. Every site of the second kind is in this page. Money shown without a Cash (a plain "%d") was not checked.

## The money text

- A Cash holds 32 bits: `Cash::Cash` 0x450a90 and `Cash::set` 0x4671a0 store one dword.
- `Cash::get_display_amount` 0x43f460 starts with `fild dword [ecx]`. After that the original works on 64 bits: the
  rounding step `cash_get_rounding_step` 0x4426e0 takes an int64 (`fild qword` at 0x4426ef), and the text function
  `cash_format_money` 0x441c10 takes an int64 (`Cash::format_display_amount` 0x450aa0 passes edx:eax at 0x450aaf).
- So only the first step is 32 bits. A screen that puts a 64-bit amount into a Cash now also gives the full amount to
  the runtime (`rt_cash_wide`), and 0x43f460 reads it from there while the Cash still holds its low word, for the
  number of reads of that site (one; the club info tab formats the balance a second time when its text is long,
  0x4639d5, and the hand-over ends at 0x4639ee).

The screens fixed this way:

| Store | Function | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 0x46399c | `club_screen_draw_info_tab` 0x460590 | balance (high word at finance +4); read twice when the text is long (0x4639c8), ended at 0x4639ee |
| 0x463a14 | same | a debt: minus the balance (`neg eax` 0x463a0a); read twice (0x463a45), ended at 0x463a6e |
| 0x463b28 | same | transfer budget; read twice (0x463b54), ended at 0x463b7a |
| 0x808ef3 | `search_screens_build_player_search_screen` 0x808690 | transfer budget |
| 0x8ea2c0 | `transfer_screens_build_bid_screen` 0x8e9760 | transfer budget. The Cash keeps the budget clamped to 2,147,483,647, because the fee colours read it again (0x8ea54c, 0x8eaf95, 0x8eb3b0, compare 0x8ea551..0x8ea559); only the text gets the full amount |
| 0x6b0366, 0x6b0399 | `manager_stats_screen_build` 0x6ae2a0 | manager money spent (+0xa4) and received (+0xac) |

A test with balances of 2.5, 5 and 12 billion shows "£2.5B", "£5B" and "£12B" on Arsenal's Finances view, and a
debt of 2.5 billion shows "(£2.5B)" (`tests/seasons/original_faults_run.py money`).

## News

A news item is 0xdf bytes: a type, a flag, 50 32-bit slots at item + 5 + 4 x slot (`NewsItem::set_slot` 0x785790,
range check 0x78579a), a date and a few fields. news.dat holds the items raw (`News::load_from_zip` reads count x
0xdf at 0x787969), and the text is written from the slots when the item is shown. Of the 2,119 calls of 0x785790,
two items carry an amount made from a 64-bit value:

- The job offer to the human (`MANAGER_MANAGER::post_job_offer_to_human_news` 0x6a4f80, type 0x1774): slot 2 is the
  offering club's full transfer budget (0x6a501c, stored 0x6a5028). Its writer 0x6a5120 reads it at 0x6a51d8 and
  formats it (Cash 0x6a533d, text 0x6a5354). Any club can offer a job, so this amount does pass 2,147,483,647.
- The finance news (`finance_post_finance_boost_news` 0x59f300, type 0x1779): slot 2 is the club's transfer budget
  (0x59f36e) after a boost, cash injection or takeover (0x59e32e, 0x59e4e2, 0x59e7bd, 0x59e907, 0x59eb1f, 0x59ed3d).
  Its writer 0x59f3e0 formats it (0x59f5a6, Cash 0x59f5c7). These events fire only below wealth band 2
  (`process_club_finance_events` jump table 0x59f2ec; band 2 is a balance above 2500 x reputation + 5 million,
  0x5986eb..0x59870d), so the budget stays far below the limit in practice, but no bound was proved.

Both now keep the clamped budget in slot 2 and, above 2,147,483,647, the full amount in slots 8 (low word) and 9
(high word), which these items do not use (slots 0 to 4 at most). The constructor clears every slot (`rep stosd`
0x77bc1b..0x77bc29), so below the limit the item and news.dat are byte for byte the same, and an old save shows
the same text. The writers take the full amount from slots 8 and 9 for their Cash. Every other money news carries a
32-bit amount: prizes and TV money (0x48e3e0, 0x48ea40), fines (0x58a738), insurance (0x58aafa), transfer and
contract news (0x4d51fb, 0x4d7975, 0x8d3e27..0x8e5351, 0x755b70), media stories (staff +0x52, 0x750721), and the
dividend, at most 2000 x reputation (0x59dc76..0x59dcf0).

## The transfer budget

`Finance::get_transfer_budget` 0x598800 (thiscall, finance record in ecx, ret 0x14) returns a 64-bit budget B in
edx:eax. Its steps: B starts as the balance (0x598807), takes the wage bill, stadium, reputation and nation terms in
64 bits (0x598b67..0x598f17), then:

1. Net spend N = this year's players bought (+0x110) less sold (+0x118), 64 bits. 0x598f4f re-extends only eax
   (`cdq`). Fixed: the high word stays (`rt_budget_hi`).
2. Q = pending fees + max(N - 8m, 0) (0x598f72..0x598f90). 0x598f8b and 0x598f92 re-extend only eax. Fixed the same
   way. B -= Q.
3. The floor R = balance - 10m (or 15m for one nation, 0x599185), kept as 32 bits in [esp+0x5c] (0x599135..0x59913d).
   Above it only a tenth of B counts: B = (B - R) x 0.1 + R (0x5991d0..0x59920e). With a balance above about
   2.157 billion, low32(balance) - 10m is negative, so a very rich club's budget fell to about a tenth. Fixed: the
   store keeps 2,147,483,647 and the runtime keeps the full R for the compare (0x5991d0, 0x5991d6) and the sum
   (0x599201).
4. The fog of war path cuts E + (B - E)/8 at 0x59979b. It needs B above about 17 billion, which no club reaches by
   2300 at the measured growth. Not changed.

The audit's "inner part" at 0x599024..0x599035 is not money: it is the club reputation squared times 0.5, left on the
x87 stack at 0x598df0.

Callers. 14 of the 37 calls keep the 64-bit result: 0x463afd and 0x463b20 (club info tab), 0x4d7541, 0x6970a3
(`__allmul`, `__alldiv`), 0x6a3b8c, 0x7b6d5f, 0x7b6e25, 0x808ee9 (player search), 0x8c0eb6, 0x8e74a7, 0x8e83a1,
0x8ea2b4 (bid screen), 0x8f4b28, 0x8f4bd5 (return addresses). The other 23 keep eax: a budget above 2,147,483,647 gave
them 0 or a wrong amount. The wrap of 0x598800 clamps the result to 2,147,483,647 for them. No fee or wage is that
large (fees are 32-bit), so the clamped budget buys anything a larger one would. The 23:

- AI: `Shortlist_Manager::allocate_transfer_budget` (0x83b74e, a negative eax gave 0: the club stopped buying),
  `try_add_player_to_club_shortlist` (0x84301b), `TRANSFER_OFFER::build_bid` (0x8e6335, fee = min(fee, budget)),
  `TRANSFER_MANAGER::check_offer_restrictions` (0x8cfe32), `handle_board_request` (0x6a8e48),
  `decide_ultimatum_response` (0x6acf9b).
- The human's bid screen: the fee colour, the fee cap and "fee = budget" (0x8ecf84, 0x8ed156, 0x8ed440, 0x8ed5a6,
  0x8ede34, 0x8ee025, 0x8ee3fa, 0x8ee622). The Offer Contract screens (0x4de3ab, 0x4de810): 0 to 5 million.
- News: the finance boost, cash injection, board takeover and job offer items (0x59e32e, 0x59e4e2, 0x59e7bd,
  0x59e907, 0x59eb1f, 0x59ed3d, 0x6a5021). The news slot keeps the clamped budget, and the text shows the full
  amount (section News).

## The top wage

`Finance::get_max_wage` 0x5968d0 takes ftol(1.3 x balance) (`fild qword` 0x5968db, the factor at 0x968de8) and keeps
only eax (0x5968ee). At 0x597019 it adds a wage term in 32 bits and divides by 52; the result is taken from the wage
bill (0x59703a). The sum wraps once 1.3 x balance plus the term passes 2,147,483,647, from a balance of about
1.65 billion, and the club's top wage goes wrong. Fixed: 0x5968e8 hands over the high word of the product, and a sum
above 2,147,483,647 is clamped to it (`rt_max_wage_sum`), so the top wage stays at about 41.3 million a week. A debt
keeps the original's result. This fix acts from about 1.65 billion, where the original's sum has already
wrapped, not from 2,147,483,647.

| The income, expenditure and profit rows and column totals of the club info tab (80 reads, 0x462cee..0x467139) | Sums of one month or one season: the month block is copied and cleared each month (0x59cc02..0x59cc78) and the year block each season (0x59cf03..0x59cf19), so they do not grow with the years. The players bought and sold totals are the only 64-bit parts. Over all 2,062 club records of the seed 6 long game (clubs outside the loaded nations too, which skip the reset at 0x59c1af..0x59c20c), the largest is 81 million in 2002, 56 million in 2011 and 30 million in 2020. Not reached. |
| `comp_util_get_club_strength_score` 0x4c0720 (balance / 300 at 0x4c0782) | Wraps from about 644 billion. Not reached. |
| Fog path 0x59979b | About 17 billion (above). Not reached. |
